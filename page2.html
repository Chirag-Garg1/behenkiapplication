What is the Lok Sabha?

The Lok Sabha, or the House of the People, is the lower house of India’s Parliament — the epicenter of law-making and democratic decision-making.

It’s where elected representatives gather to debate, discuss, and pass legislation that shapes the future of over a billion people.

In this committee, MPs (Members of Parliament) tackle issues like:

  • National legislation and constitutional amendments
  • Public welfare schemes and governance reforms
  • Social, economic, and environmental justice
  • Policy accountability and national interest

The Lok Sabha is structured, formal, and driven by procedure, logic, and purpose — but it’s also a battlefield of ideas and ideologies.

What do YOU do in MUN as a Lok Sabha delegate?

You take on the role of a Member of Parliament — representing a constituency and a political ideology.

Your mission?

  • Debate bills that will become laws
  • Raise questions, motions, and amendments
  • Represent the voice of your people and party
  • Strike a balance between politics and public interest

It’s not just about debating. It’s about shaping laws that touch lives. It’s about standing tall as a lawmaker, leader, and patriot in the heart of Indian democracy.
