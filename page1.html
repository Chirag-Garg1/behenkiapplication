What is the AIPPM?

The All India Political Parties Meet (AIPPM) is a simulation of the informal gathering of India's major political parties. It's where leaders debate, negotiate, and try to reach consensus on pressing national issues.

In this committee, participants:

  • Represent real Indian political parties
  • Debate on current and relevant national topics
  • Negotiate alliances and strategies
  • Experience the art of coalition politics

The AIPPM is about understanding India's political landscape and learning how democracy works through dialogue and debate.

What do YOU do in MUN as an AIPPM delegate?

You step into the shoes of a political leader, voicing your party's stance and negotiating with others.

Your job?

  • Present your party's viewpoint with conviction
  • Engage in passionate yet respectful debate
  • Form alliances and build consensus
  • Draft resolutions that reflect collective agreement

AIPPM is about persuasive speaking, strategic thinking, and real-world political experience.
